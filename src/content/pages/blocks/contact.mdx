title: Contact
description: Contact sections for project inquiries, support, and sales conversations.
---

Each block shows your contact details and renders its default slot in a form
panel. Put your own form in the slot, built from Form, Field, Input and
Textarea, and point it at your form handler. The blocks do not provide a
submission backend. Without slot content, the block shows only the contact
details. Give each field an ID that is unique on the page.
