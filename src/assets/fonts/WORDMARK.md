# Fulldev Wordmark

`fulldev-wordmark.woff2` is the approved logo-only derivative of the
repository's Geist variable font (font version 1.800), under `OFL.txt`.
It is a static weight 600, with ASCII and the ss02, ss03, ss06 and ss08
alternates. Render at 20px: outer corners have a 0.5px circular fillet,
inner corners 0.25px. Overlaps were removed before rounding; advance widths
and kerning are preserved. Use the original Geist for all other text.
